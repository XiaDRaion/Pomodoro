# 🍅 Pomodoro

A simple and customizable Pomodoro Timer designed to help you manage your time, stay focused, and work more effectively.

The **Pomodoro Technique** is a time-management method that breaks your work into focused intervals, traditionally **25 minutes of work followed by a short break**. This application makes it easy to organize those intervals and keep track of your working sessions.

---

## ⏱️ Features

- 🍅 **Pomodoro Timer** — Set your own work and break intervals.
- 🎵 **YouTube Integration** — Listen to your favorite music while working by pasting a YouTube link into the input field and clicking the play button.
- 🖼️ **Custom Backgrounds** — Choose from several available background images or upload your own to personalize your workspace.
- 🎨 **Personalized Experience** — Create a workspace that feels comfortable and helps you stay focused.

---

## 🎵 Listen While You Work

Need some background music while working?

Simply paste a **YouTube link** into the input field and click the **Play** button. Your music will play alongside the timer so you can stay in the zone. 🎧

---

## 🖼️ Customize Your Background

Make your Pomodoro sessions a little more enjoyable by customizing the background.

You can choose from several built-in images or **upload your own image** to create a workspace that matches your mood. ✨

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

Set your work interval, start the timer, and get to work!

When the timer ends, take a short break before starting your next session. Repeat the cycle and keep making progress, one session at a time. 🍅
